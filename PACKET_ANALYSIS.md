# Packet Analysis

## Why packet captures were used

Throughput summaries show the outcome of a simulation, but packet captures help verify that expected traffic types actually existed. The project therefore generated PCAPs and inspected them with Wireshark.

## Station / client capture

The retained report screenshot shows:

- UDP application traffic,
- 802.11 management traffic,
- Beacon frames associated with the simulated infrastructure network.

This supports the presence of normal Wi-Fi traffic at the Station side.

## Access Point capture

The AP-side capture is described as showing:

- periodic Beacon activity advertising the wireless network,
- application-layer data traffic.

## Interference-node capture

When the interference condition is enabled, the retained capture shows repeated UDP broadcast traffic generated by the third node.

That is the strongest packet-level evidence for the implemented interference mechanism.

## Evidence boundary

Wireshark confirms packet behavior visible in PCAP. It does not independently prove:

- physical RF energy level,
- SNR degradation,
- continuous-wave RF interference,
- real-world spectrum occupancy.

Those would require different instrumentation or PHY-layer measurements.
