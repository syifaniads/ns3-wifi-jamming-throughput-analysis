# AI Usage Disclosure

The retained academic report explicitly declares use of **Google Gemini Pro** during the original coursework period (20–29 November 2025).

According to that declaration, generative AI was used for assistance with:

- initial NS-3 simulation-code scaffolding,
- debugging command-line argument handling,
- discussing interpretation of the 80 m throughput result,
- generating a Python/Matplotlib plotting script.

## Portfolio policy

This repository does not hide that tooling assistance. At the same time, it does not treat AI-generated suggestions as independent evidence.

Engineering claims in this portfolio are grounded in retained report artifacts such as:

- configuration/code excerpts,
- measured throughput values,
- terminal output screenshots,
- NetAnim visualization,
- PCAP/Wireshark screenshots,
- documented experiment automation.

## Why this is documented

Transparent tooling disclosure is preferable to implying that every original line was produced manually. The portfolio focuses on the ability to design, run, validate, interpret, and communicate the experiment while preserving accurate attribution.
